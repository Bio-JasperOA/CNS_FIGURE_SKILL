# Literature-to-interpretation logic

These records separate retrieved article content from our reusable interpretation rules. Indexed text was sufficient for the stated narrow patterns; no complete-paper or image-level review is claimed where only excerpts/abstracts were accessible. Dates and bibliographic metadata were checked on 2026-09-17. The older Science/Cell papers are foundational examples, not presented as the newest work.

The rules are a synthesis, not proof that every CNS article uses one narrative. The title of a journal does not upgrade evidence quality.

## P1 — A human embryonic limb cell atlas resolved in space and time
Nature; 2023 online; 2024 issue. DOI: 10.1038/s41586-023-06806-x. [Source](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06806-x)

**Read scope:** indexed Results, Discussion, Methods and Fig. 4 legend excerpts.

State atlas → measured spatial localization → nominated regulator → MSC knockdown followed by myocyte-gene readout. Separate molecular consequence from a complete in vivo developmental mechanism.

## P2 — Spatially organized multicellular immune hubs in human colorectal cancer
Cell; 2021. DOI: 10.1016/j.cell.2021.08.003. [Source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34450029/)

**Read scope:** PubMed abstract and publisher-indexed abstract/highlights; full publisher page returned 403.

Across-tumor co-variation of programmes is followed by spatial localization of candidate multicellular hubs. Localization strengthens anatomical interpretation without being an intervention.

## P3 — Lineage tracing on transcriptional landscapes links state to fate during differentiation
Science; 2020. DOI: 10.1126/science.aaw3381. [Source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7608074/)

**Read scope:** indexed Results and Fig. 2 legend excerpts; direct PMC open encountered browser challenge.

Measured clonal links connect earlier transcriptional states with later fate. The interpretation explicitly depends on tested sister-cell similarity, illustrating why state and fate are not interchangeable.

## P4 — Transfer learning enables predictions in network biology
Nature; 2023. DOI: 10.1038/s41586-023-06139-9. [Source](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06139-9)

**Read scope:** indexed abstract, figure titles, extended-data legends and contributions; not full main text.

Distinguish representation/prediction evidence from the separately reported experimental validation in cardiac microtissues. In silico perturbation must not be relabelled as a performed wet-lab intervention.

## P5 — Confronting false discoveries in single-cell differential expression
Nature Communications; 2021. DOI: 10.1038/s41467-021-25960-2. [Source](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-021-25960-2)

**Read scope:** indexed Results, Discussion, Methods and availability text.

The study challenges an initially plausible explanation, tests biological-replicate handling and uses orthogonal in situ evidence. Preserve alternatives, specimen-level uncertainty and negative controls rather than selecting the method with the most discoveries.

## P6 — Human lung cancer harbors spatially organized stem-immunity hubs associated with response to immunotherapy
Nature Immunology; 2024. DOI: 10.1038/s41590-024-01792-2. [Source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/12096941)

**Read scope:** indexed abstract and first Results subsection.

Pretreatment tissue organization is associated with later therapy outcome. Retain the observational treatment-response scope rather than claiming the tissue hub was proven to cause response.

## P7 — Deep-learning-based gene perturbation effect prediction does not yet outperform simple linear baselines
Nature Methods; 2025. DOI: 10.1038/s41592-025-02772-6. [Source](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-025-02772-6)

**Read scope:** indexed Main, Methods, limitations and figure legends.

Compare prediction targets with matched simple baselines and inspect systematic errors. The negative result is task- and dataset-scoped; it does not prove that all biological AI is ineffective.

## P8 — Scientists rise up against statistical significance
Nature Comment; 2019. DOI: 10.1038/d41586-019-00857-9. [Source](https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-019-00857-9)

**Read scope:** indexed introduction and bibliographic details only; conceptual commentary, not an experiment.

The opening critiques interpreting a nonsignificant result as no difference. Use this as a conceptual caution, not as empirical evidence for a biological claim.

## R1 — Revealing gene function with statistical inference at single-cell resolution
Nature Reviews Genetics; 2024. DOI: 10.1038/s41576-024-00750-w. [Source](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41576-024-00750-w)

**Read scope:** indexed abstract only; navigation reference.

Review-level context connecting molecular profiling, perturbations, cohorts and phenotype. Detailed rules in this module are author-defined and require the project-specific methods, not abstract-only authority.

## R2 — The diversification of methods for studying cell–cell interactions and communication
Nature Reviews Genetics; 2024. DOI: 10.1038/s41576-023-00685-8. [Source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38238518/)

**Read scope:** PubMed metadata and available indexed review introduction; navigation reference.

Read the relevant measurement and computational sections when interpreting communication. Do not treat one ligand–receptor inference as equivalent to every experimental class of communication evidence.

## Operational lesson

Link a claim to the experiment that tests it. Separate convergent descriptions, orthogonal measurement, intervention and independent replication. A strong Results section advances a question through evidence, including findings that revise the proposed model. It does not need to escalate every observational study into a causal story.
