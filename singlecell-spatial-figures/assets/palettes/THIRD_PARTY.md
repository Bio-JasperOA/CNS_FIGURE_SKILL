# Third-party palette attribution

This catalogue records color values and their source evidence, not a claim of original authorship or a new license for upstream projects. Repository visibility is not a grant of a repository-wide open-source license.

- Matplotlib color tables: Matplotlib contributors / John D. Hunter. Source and license: https://github.com/matplotlib/matplotlib/blob/v3.10.8/LICENSE/LICENSE . ColorBrewer designs: Cynthia Brewer, Mark Harrower and Pennsylvania State University, https://colorbrewer2.org/ .
- Seaborn Mako, Vlag, Icefire and colorblind definitions: Michael Waskom and contributors (MIT), https://github.com/mwaskom/seaborn/blob/v0.13.2/LICENSE .
- Plotly / CARTOColor supplemental maps: Plotly contributors (MIT), https://github.com/plotly/plotly.py/blob/v6.5.2/LICENSE.txt ; CARTOColor (CC BY 3.0), https://github.com/CartoDB/CartoColor .
- C14-C17 follow the sequences exposed by Seurat's DiscretePalette, which credits Kevin Wright's pals package and GPL-3. The source/credit is retained; the Seurat function is not copied. https://github.com/satijalab/seurat/blob/83f9659912bd5fffdd65e45997236e4bd14e6284/R/visualization.R .
- C19-C20 are the first 100 entries of Colorcet Glasbey sequences; Colorcet contributors / Peter Kovesi, https://github.com/holoviz/colorcet . Their author-software references are in sources.json. The frozen definition is https://github.com/holoviz/colorcet/blob/9f23c9659e81a3bd9b23c7bf874d5c9323f07c16/colorcet/__init__.py (blob f2a34149394d971e0092d1275233424607954f7d): C19 uses glasbey_bw_minc_20_maxl_70[0:100], C20 uses glasbey_bw_minc_20[0:100].
- C11-C13, C18 and the remaining scientific-package references retain the origin descriptions in index.json; these are not claims that the scientific authors invented the color schemes.
- C21 is a derived trial from the review package, generated by deterministic CIELAB greedy maximin with a navy seed. It is not extracted author color data or an accessibility-certified palette.

The original color-review build environments were Matplotlib 3.10.8, Seaborn 0.13.2 and Plotly 6.5.2. Continuous entries here are their reviewed RGB8 samples; the runtime does not download upstream code. No font binaries or original paper texts are included.
